## About

Red Planet Rampage is a multiplayer first-person shooter made by the game development group at Hackerspace NTNU, built in Unity and C#. It was released on Steam in 2024 and won Gamers' Choice at the Norwegian Game Awards 2024.

## My part

- The menus and the player lobby
- The player movement system, including a fix that stops players getting stuck in terrain
- Bug fixing, code maintenance and further development
- Working in a cross-disciplinary team, using agile methods and a shared Git codebase
