## About

A mobile, IoT-based system for tracking participants during ultra-trail races, developed as a team for our bachelor thesis at NTNU. Data from IoT devices goes to a C# backend over MQTT, and SignalR pushes real-time updates to a React and TypeScript frontend for three user groups.

## My part

- Mainly the embedded and IoT side, programmed in C on the Zephyr real-time operating system
- Communication between the IoT devices and the backend over MQTT
- Debugging across the whole chain, from embedded and network to backend and frontend
